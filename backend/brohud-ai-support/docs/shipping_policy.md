# Shipping Policy

## Standard Shipping

- Orders are processed within 1–2 business days.
- Standard delivery usually takes 3–7 business days depending on the delivery location.
- Customers receive a tracking number once the order is shipped.

## Express Shipping

- Express shipping is available for selected locations.
- Delivery typically takes 1–3 business days.

## Delayed Shipments

Shipping may be delayed due to:

- Public holidays
- Weather conditions
- High order volume
- Courier partner delays

## Order Tracking

Customers can track their order using their Order ID.

## Incorrect Address

If an incorrect shipping address is provided, contact customer support immediately before the order is dispatched.

## Contact Support

If your order has not arrived within the expected delivery period, please contact customer support with your Order ID.
