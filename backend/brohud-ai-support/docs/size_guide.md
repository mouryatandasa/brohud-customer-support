# Size Guide

## Hoodie Sizes

| Size | Chest | Length |
| ---- | ----- | ------ |
| S    | 38 in | 27 in  |
| M    | 40 in | 28 in  |
| L    | 42 in | 29 in  |
| XL   | 44 in | 30 in  |
| XXL  | 46 in | 31 in  |

## Fit

* Oversized Hoodies → Choose your regular size.
* Regular Fit → Choose your normal size.
* Slim Fit → Consider one size larger for comfort.

## Need Help?

If you're unsure about sizing, contact customer support before placing your order.
