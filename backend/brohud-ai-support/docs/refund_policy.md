# Refund Policy

## Eligibility

Customers can request a refund within **7 days** of receiving their order.

## Refund Conditions

A refund is available only if:

* The product is unused.
* Original tags are attached.
* The item is returned in its original packaging.
* The product is not damaged by the customer.

## Non-Refundable Items

Refunds are **not** available for:

* Used or washed products
* Gift cards
* Clearance or final sale items
* Products damaged due to customer misuse

## Refund Process

1. Submit a refund request through customer support.
2. Our team reviews the request within **2 business days**.
3. Once approved, the refund is processed.
4. The amount is credited to the original payment method within **5–7 business days**.

## Exchange Policy

Customers may request an exchange instead of a refund if the desired size or color is available.

## Contact Support

If you need help with a refund, please contact our customer support team with your Order ID.
