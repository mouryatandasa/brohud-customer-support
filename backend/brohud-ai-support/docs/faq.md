# Frequently Asked Questions

## What payment methods are accepted?

We accept UPI, Credit Cards, Debit Cards, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (available in selected locations).

---

## How can I track my order?

Use your Order ID to track your shipment or contact customer support.

---

## Can I cancel my order?

Orders can only be cancelled before they are shipped.

---

## How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3–7 business days.
Express delivery takes 1–3 business days.

---

## How do I request a refund?

Contact customer support within 7 days of receiving your order.

---

## How do I contact support?

Reach out through our website chat or email with your Order ID.
